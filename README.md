# Introduction
VANJEE WLR-722 is a 32-line LiDAR designed for intelligent equipment, robots, and low-speed autonomous driving scenarios. It adopts the TOF ranging principle and built-in 6-axis IMU, with a 40m ranging capability and a ranging accuracy of up to ±2cm. The LiDAR adopts an ultra wide angle scanning design, which can effectively identify suspended objects and low obstacles, helping to achieve seamless environmental perception in all scenarios. Yahboom has provided customized adapter cables (1 to 3) for it, supporting RJ45 network connectors, DC5521 power sockets, and GPS interface sockets to meet different development and usage scenarios. Compatible with ROS robot operating system, compatible with Autoware. Universe, can directly import configuration files, and quickly achieve mapping and navigation.
# Features
* Adopting a 32-line spherical design, providing a wide field of view of 62°(vertical) x 360°(horizontal). Its ranging distance can reach 40m with an accuracy of ±2cm, providing comprehensive environmental perception for robots and low-speed autonomous vehicles.
* Equipped with 6-axis IMU, it can automatically correct point cloud distortion caused by vehicle acceleration, deceleration, or uneven terrain. Even in dynamic movements such as climbing or turning, this ensures reliable map and navigation performance.
* Yahboom provides a customized "1 to 3" adapter cable. Equipped with RJ45 network connector, DC5521 power socket, and GPS interface port. This design allows you to quickly connect LiDAR to your preferred controller or embedded platform.
* Compatible with ROS operating system, Yahboom provides multiple mapping and navigation routines, and directly supports Autoware.Universe, greatly reducing the time to market for robotics and autonomous driving projects.
